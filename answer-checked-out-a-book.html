The Founding Harbor Card #10234 — Local History
The confession

My name is Walter Hennessey. Margo rejected my local history from this year's Local Voices lineup — not for the writing. For the facts. Three founding dates I refused to correct, because they matched the version I'd been selling in my "rare" first editions online for years. She recognized my own fiction in both places at once, and she was going to say so.

I've been a patron since the library's very first year open — card number 10234, the lowest ever issued. Margo used to bring that up fondly. That night, I grabbed a book on my way to the Rare Books room, so I'd look like I was there for the showcase like everyone else.

The book was The Founding Harbor. I never got the chance to read it.
